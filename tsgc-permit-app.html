Enter Your API Key
This app uses the Anthropic AI to read your floor plans. You need a free API key to use it.

How to get your key:
1. Go to console.anthropic.com
2. Sign up for a free account
3. Click "API Keys" in the left menu
4. Click "Create Key" and copy it
5. Paste it below — it starts with sk-ant-

Your key is saved only on this computer and never sent anywhere except to Anthropic.
